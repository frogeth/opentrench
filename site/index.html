Every call from your groups, in one terminal.

opentrench watches the Discord servers and Telegram chats you already sit in and turns every contract address into a live card: who called it, market cap then and now, holder security, one-click buys. Add tweets from J7, pings from people who @ you, and columns you arrange yourself.

What it does

Nothing leaves your computer except the requests to Discord, Telegram and the price APIs. No account with us, no server of ours in the middle.

Calls, not scrollback

One card per token, newest call first: caller, channel, first-call badge, market cap at the call and the multiple since, age, liquidity, volume, the buys/sells bar and holder security (top 10, dev, insiders, LP lock).

Columns you arrange

Calls, chats, Top Callers, Cove, Salpha, J7. Scope each to any set of channels, filter them, drag to reorder, resize, give each its own alert sound. Layouts persist.

Pings, with context

Everyone who @s you across Discord and Telegram lands in one panel with the messages before and after, a jump to the chat, and a reply box.

J7 tweets, paired with launches

Stream J7Tracker natively. Tweets that mention your calls get a strip; pump.fun and Pons tokens launched off a tweet appear under it within seconds. Star an account to get pinged when it tweets.

Buy without leaving

Cove buy buttons on every card, Cove and Salpha conversations as columns, right-click any contract to buy, research, or open the drill-down with a chart and every caller pinned on it.

Send from the terminal

Reply in Telegram from any column. On Discord, sending goes through a small plugin in your own Discord app, so there is no token and no self-bot.

The drill-down

Click a token's picture, or right-click any contract, called or not.

How Discord is connected

No token, no self-bot. opentrench reads and sends Discord through the opentrench plugin for Vencord, which runs inside your own Discord app and uses Discord's own functions. To Discord it is simply you using Discord. Set it up once in about five minutes: Mac and Windows guide.

Already using a token from an earlier version? It keeps reading, read-only. Switch whenever you like.
